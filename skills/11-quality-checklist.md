# 11. Quality checklist (check before delivering)

## Story
- [ ] The problem is visible within the first second.
- [ ] Every problem appears on screen before the narrator names it.
- [ ] Each fix causes or exposes the next problem.
- [ ] Ends on a question to the viewer.

## Picture (look at a contact sheet of ~12 frames across the video)
- [ ] The hero object looks identical in every shot.
- [ ] Nothing important is off-screen, cut off at the edge, or hidden behind a prop.
- [ ] Cutaways read clearly: surface line, hidden parts and ground all visible.
- [ ] No visible seams (sky showing through underground areas, z-fighting, objects floating above surfaces).
- [ ] Subject sits in the upper-middle and away from the right-edge buttons.
- [ ] 1080×1920, 30 fps, the length the brief asked for.

## Captions
- [ ] 2–5 words each, one yellow word per sentence, no line wraps.
- [ ] Never covering the key detail of the shot (move up where needed).
- [ ] Sync verified by transcription; no word leaks across boundaries.

## Audio
- [ ] Every line fits its slot; key words land on their visual moment.
- [ ] Voice clearly above the effects; no music unless asked.
- [ ] Effects match visible events; −14 LUFS.

## Hand-off
- [ ] Say plainly what's approximate (voice match vs clone, estimated timings) and anything you changed from the brief.
- [ ] Deliver the final MP4 to where the creator wants it; keep the caption-free version for later edits.
